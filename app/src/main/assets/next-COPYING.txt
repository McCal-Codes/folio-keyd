The next-word lists in this folder (next-*.txt) are derived from the example
sentences of Tatoeba.

  Source: Tatoeba contributors, https://tatoeba.org
  Exports: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/ (eng, deu, spa,
           fra, ita, por), weekly export of 2026-09-26
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR)
           https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/

The data was modified: the sentences are not included. Nine in ten of them
were counted into pairs of consecutive words, the pairs were limited to words
in Keyd's own word lists and cut to about twenty thousand per language, and
only which words follow which, in order of how often, is kept. The script
that does this is scripts/bigrams.py in Keyd's source.
